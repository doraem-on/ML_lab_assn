🌾 DhanMitra – Empowering Rural India with AI-Driven Financial Literacy
Bridging the 73% Financial Literacy Gap in Rural India through Multilingual, Offline AI Assistance.

🚨 Problem Statement
India’s rural population faces a massive financial literacy crisis:
📉 Only 27% financial literacy rate
🌾 Farmers denied KCC loans due to lack of awareness
🏭 MSMEs stalled by documentation confusion
👩‍🎓 Youth unaware of SIPs, APY, PMMY & government schemes
📶 Low internet connectivity blocks digital access
🤝 Middlemen exploitation due to information gaps
This results in credit gaps, missed benefits, delayed claims, and persistent poverty cycles.

💡 Proposed Solution – DhanMitra
DhanMitra is a multilingual conversational AI platform built to empower rural India with instant, personalized financial guidance.
It provides:
🗣 Voice + Text interaction in local dialects
📲 Offline functionality for no-network villages
🎯 Personalized government scheme guidance
📊 Application simulations
📌 Deadline alerts (PMFBY, KCC renewals, etc.)
🔍 Verified RBI & NABARD-backed information
Example Query:
“Bhojpuri: Crop loan kaise milega?”
DhanMitra instantly explains:
Eligibility criteria
Required documents
Application steps
Nearby banking options

🧠 Core Innovation

🔹 Multilingual NLU Engine
Fine-tuned IndicBERT
Dialect-aware adaptation (Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, etc.)
85%+ multilingual understanding accuracy

🔹 RAG-Based Financial Knowledge Engine
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
Verified data from:
RBI circulars
SEBI updates
Government scheme portals
Regulation-aligned, hallucination-free responses

🔹 Offline & Low-Bandwidth Support
Quantized ONNX models (~1GB)
On-device question answering
Auto-sync when internet resumes
Optimized for low-end smartphones

🔹 Cultural & Seasonal Intelligence
Monsoon crop insurance alerts
Festival-based savings planning
Seasonal loan advisory reminders
